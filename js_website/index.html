ABOUT JOAN SINCLAIR

Helping families
find clarity and
peace during hard times.

For over three decades, I have supported individuals and families through some of life’s most challenging transitions. I believe that even in moments of deep conflict, people can find respectful, lasting solutions when they are guided with compassion and skill.

My approach is collaborative and non-judgmental. I help clients communicate more effectively, understand each other’s perspectives, and create agreements that protect what matters most — especially the well-being of children.

THE PROCESS

How we work together

01
Free Consultation

We start with a confidential 30-minute call to discuss your situation and see if mediation or counselling is the right path forward.

02
Individual Meetings

We meet separately first so each person can speak openly and feel heard before we move into joint conversations.

03
Joint Sessions & Agreements

We work together (with caucusing when helpful) to reach practical agreements. I prepare clear documents for you and your lawyers.

All sessions are completely confidential. Mediation is conducted in a closed format.
